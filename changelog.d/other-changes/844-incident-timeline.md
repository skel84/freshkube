- **Application timeline:** an application's page has a Timeline tab that lists, newest first, what happened to it in the last 3 h (6, 12 or 24 h on request): deploys, Kubernetes Warning events, pod restarts and node changes from the open cluster. A chip for each source shows its count, or why it wasn't read; Kubernetes events are cut where the cluster stops keeping them and the list says "not kept by the cluster" there, and a quiet stretch names every source it read. Kargo promotions, Argo CD syncs and Coroot incidents show as not read yet outside example data. It is read-only and reads only while the tab shows ([docs/features/applications.md](docs/features/applications.md)).
