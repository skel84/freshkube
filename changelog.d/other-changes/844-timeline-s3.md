- **Application Timeline, Promotions and Incidents:** an application's Timeline tab now reads its Kargo Promotions from the open cluster and Coroot's incidents for its workloads. The Promotions chip lists each Promotion that finished in the window, or is still running, and says what it could not see (a Kargo Project read through another connection, a listing cut at the page cap, a refused or unserved listing). The Incidents chip lists Coroot's incidents for the application's workloads that overlap the window, and reads "not read" with why when Coroot isn't connected, isn't linked to the cluster, has no project, is unreachable or refuses. Coroot's list is a bounded sample, so Incidents never counts as a quiet hour. Read-only.
