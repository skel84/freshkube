- **Delivery links say where their evidence came from:** each link of a
  delivery trail now lists the observations behind it, with the cluster,
  object, UID, resource version, field and whether it was reported or
  declared, and a trail's text prints one `from …` line for each. What the
  join itself concluded is marked as such and named by its rule. A link from
  a source that couldn't be read lists none. Where a confirmed link rests
  on a declared field alone on one side, its evidence shows only that. The
  read time comes from the caller. No link's confidence changes.
