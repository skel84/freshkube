- **Delivery links say where their evidence came from:** each link of a
  delivery trail now lists the observations behind it, with the cluster,
  object, UID, resource version, field and whether it was reported,
  declared or derived, and a trail's text prints one `from …` line for
  each. A confirmed link stands on a reported or derived observation on
  each side, and a link from a source that couldn't be read lists none.
  The read time comes from the caller. No link's confidence changes.
