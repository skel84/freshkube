- **Team setup:** your team now survives a restart. Settings › Team setup keeps the source, the version you reviewed, which of your contexts each team cluster is and your own values beside your preferences (never in `freshkube.yaml`), "Use the team's value" takes one of your values back out of your own `freshkube.yaml`, and Join takes an optional token for an https:// file, kept in your key store only. While joining, the review says "Review the team’s setup before joining".
