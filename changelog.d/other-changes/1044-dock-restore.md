- **Dock tabs of other clusters are kept:** quitting with log or shell tabs open on several clusters and starting on one of them no longer drops the others from the saved dock. The file keeps at most 8 log and 8 shell tabs per cluster and 64 in all, and a cluster not shown for 30 days loses its saved tabs.
