- **Tabs:** open several places of the open cluster at once in a strip under the header, each with its own back and forward history. Command-T duplicates the view, Command-W closes it, Shift-Command-T reopens the last closed one, and Command-Shift-] and [ step through them. Command-click on a link opens it in a background tab, Shift-Command-click in front, and Command-Return in the search palette. The tabs come back at the next start, as views alone. Option-Command-] and [ now switch the tabs of a pane or a node ([docs/features/tabs.md](docs/features/tabs.md)).
