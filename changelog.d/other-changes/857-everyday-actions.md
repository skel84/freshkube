- **Everyday actions:** Scale a Deployment, StatefulSet or ReplicaSet, Restart a Deployment, StatefulSet or DaemonSet, and Suspend or resume a CronJob from the Resources row menu, its keys (S, R, P) and the toolbar, never from a button on a row. Each goes through the write framework: the access review, the version check, a server-side dry run, a write with field manager `freshkube`, the observed outcome and the audit log. Scale and Suspend offer Undo; a Restart cannot be taken back. A read-only cluster shows them disabled with its reason, and an object Argo CD or Flux manages says that self-heal may put the change back.
